Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I choose the right air purifier?

A: Consider your room size and whether you need it for allergies, smoke, or dust.

Q: How often should I replace the filter?

A: Most HEPA filters need replacement every 6-12 months.

Q: Do air purifiers use a lot of electricity?

A: Most modern purifiers are energy efficient and cost very little to run.

Q: Can air purifiers help with pet allergies?

A: Yes, HEPA filters can capture pet dander and reduce allergy symptoms.

Q: How loud are air purifiers?

A: Noise levels vary, but many have quiet settings for nighttime use.

Q: Do I need an air purifier in every room?

A: It depends on your needs, but most people start with bedrooms and main living areas.

Q: How do I know what size purifier I need?

A: Check the CADR rating and match it to your room's square footage.

Q: Can air purifiers remove odors?

A: Yes, many have carbon filters that help eliminate smells from cooking, pets, or smoke.

Q: How much maintenance do air purifiers require?

A: Mainly filter changes and occasional cleaning of the exterior.

Q: Are air purifiers safe to run 24/7?

A: Yes, most are designed for continuous operation.
